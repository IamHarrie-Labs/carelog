# CareLog

Approved direction: the reference-led editorial concept with sage controls and a plain cream canvas. The photographic surround was explicitly rejected as part of the website.

- Background: #FFFFEF; workspace: #F8F8EE; forest: #193C34; sage action: #D2E5CE.
- Marketing headlines: Libre Caslon Text, regular and its own italic.
- Wordmark, navigation, forms and all app records: DM Sans.
- Self-hosted fonts with OFL licenses live in public/assets.
- The single emblem master is public/assets/carelog-logo.svg. Reuse it, including the favicon. Do not generate new marks per screen.
- Hero: centered headline and sage action followed by a staggered example conversation and two slim record slips. No backdrop photograph, decorative vines, paper textures, chart or dashboard hero.
- Care data stays attributable and honest. An acknowledgement never closes unfinished work. A missing record is not proof care did not happen.
- Authenticated interface: forest sidebar on desktop, horizontally scrollable section navigation on mobile, warm cream workspace, readable sans-serif records and restrained borders.
- Copy: plain and calm; no invented testimonials, clinical safety guarantees or compliance claims.

The landing page links to #/login and #/signup. Stable form and workflow test IDs remain in place.

## Motion and section color

Use the approved cream hero followed by moss (#E1E6CD), peach (#F2DCC9), eucalyptus (#DCE9E2) and the forest closing section (#193C34). The hero explains a handoff with finite, replayable motion. Workflow previews change on scroll or direct selection; family examples use keyboard-accessible tabs. Reduced-motion mode shows the complete content without positional animation. No scroll hijacking, endless decorative motion or moving clinical records.
